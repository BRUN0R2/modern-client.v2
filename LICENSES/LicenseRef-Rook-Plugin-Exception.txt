0. Definitions.

"Engine" means Modern-Client.v2, or any work based on it, distributed under the
GPLv3 (or a later version) along with this Exception.

"Plugin Interface" means the means by which the Engine loads and runs plugins:
the Rook bytecode format, the plugin and mod manifest formats, the API
declarations the Engine publishes for plugins (".rookd" files), the Rook
standard library, and the messages the Engine exchanges with plugins at run
time through these interfaces.

"Plugin" means a work, in source or compiled form, together with its data,
assets and configuration, that is designed to be loaded and run by the Engine
and that interacts with the Engine only through the Plugin Interface. A mod
made of Plugins and assets is a Plugin for the purpose of this Exception.

"Native Extension" means code that interacts with the Engine by any means other
than the Plugin Interface, including native code loaded into the Engine's
process through its native extension interface.

1. Grant of Additional Permission.

You have permission to create, run, propagate and convey Plugins under terms of
your choice, and to have the Engine load and run them, even though the Engine
runs a Plugin inside its own process and exchanges data with it through the
Plugin Interface. A Plugin does not become a work based on the Engine, and is
not subject to the terms of the GPLv3, merely because it is loaded and run by
the Engine or written against the Plugin Interface.

You may also convey the Engine together with Plugins, provided that the Engine
itself, including any modification of it, remains under the GPLv3 along with
this Exception.

2. Limits.

This Exception does not grant any permission for:

  a) a modified version of the Engine, or any work based on the Engine other
     than a Plugin, which remains subject to all terms of the GPLv3;

  b) a Native Extension, which remains subject to all terms of the GPLv3 as far
     as it is a work based on the Engine;

  c) code of the Engine copied into a Plugin, which remains subject to the
     terms of its own license.

3. Modified versions.

If you modify the Engine, you may extend this Exception to your version, but you
are not obligated to do so. As permitted by section 7 of the GPLv3, you may
remove this Exception from your copies of the Engine or of a modified version;
if you do, delete the corresponding notices.
